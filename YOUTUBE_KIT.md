# YouTube kit — "Privatizing a country on-chain"

Everything for one 3–4 minute video: script, title, description, tags,
chapters and thumbnail (`brand/youtube-thumbnail-1280x720.png`).

How to record: a screen recording of the site and MetaMask with your voice
over it. On a Mac: **Shift + Cmd + 5 → Record Entire Screen** (choose your
microphone under Options). On Windows: **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or the
free OBS Studio. No editing needed — if you make a mistake, pause and repeat
the sentence; you can trim later in YouTube Studio's built-in editor.

## Script (≈ 3½ minutes)

**[0:00 — Hook] Show: the site's home page**
> In the 1990s, many post-Soviet countries privatized their state companies
> with vouchers. In a few years, most of the wealth ended up in a few hands.
> I wanted to know: does that happen because of human nature — or because of
> the order in which things were done? So I rebuilt the process on a
> blockchain, where every step is public.

**[0:25 — What it is] Show: the white paper page**
> This is Sakartvelo Exchange — a simulation of the privatization process on
> Arbitrum. The process is real: tokens, auctions, elections and treasuries
> all run on public smart contracts. Only the state assets are simulated.

**[0:45 — Claim] Show: /live, connect MetaMask, Verify, Claim**
> Every citizen claims 1,000 INVEST — the voucher of this system. You only pay
> gas. Your wallet needs to hold a small amount of ETH, which is checked but
> not spent.

**[1:15 — Auctions] Show: a company card, place a bid**
> With INVEST you bid for company shares in round auctions. Shares are split
> in proportion to the bids: the lowest bid sets the price of one share, and
> everyone gets shares in proportion to what they bid. Each share is an NFT in
> your wallet.

**[1:50 — Governance] Show: company 11 card, candidates, vote**
> Once half of a company's shares are sold, shareholders elect a governor.
> Candidates publish their program first, and you need 51% to win —
> otherwise there's a runoff.

**[2:20 — Treasury] Show: the treasury tabs**
> The governor runs the company treasury, but with limits: only 5% per term
> without asking; bigger payments need a shareholder vote; dividends are voted
> at the end of each term.

**[2:45 — The experiment] Show: the experiment note on GitHub**
> Here's the twist. My research says vouchers should only become tradable
> after the companies are sold. By accident, trading opened early in the live
> system. So I've turned it into an open experiment: will ownership spread out,
> or concentrate in a few hands — just like in the 90s? The hypotheses are
> written down in advance, and the results will be published.

**[3:15 — Call to action] Show: the site**
> You can take part — link in the description. Claim your INVEST, buy shares,
> run for governor. This is research, not financial advice. Let's see who ends
> up owning the country.
